# Code of conduct

DutyGate adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short, we want a harassment-free experience for everyone: be kind,
be constructive, assume good faith, and respect differing views and experiences.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when you
represent the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting
(the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, marking it as a conduct
report), or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that don't follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior is inappropriate, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
